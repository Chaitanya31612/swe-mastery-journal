# 9. Pendo: measure meaningful product behavior

**Beginner · about 2 hours with practice**

Define a product event and separate analytics identity, consent/data policy, and business success.

## Use it

Understanding feature adoption and operational task funnels.

**Benefit:** Product behavior visibility and guidance workflows.

**Cost or limit:** Instrumentation meaning, missing data, identity, and privacy need ownership.
