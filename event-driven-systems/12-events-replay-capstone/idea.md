## Understand the idea

Replay is a tool for a purpose. Rebuilding a reporting projection from retained facts can be useful.

Re-sending every email or charging every payment again is a different and often unacceptable effect. Separate rebuildable projections from irreversible side-effect workflows.

A new group gets independent progress, but an existing inbox for the same consumer identity may intentionally skip old event IDs.

To rebuild, use a deliberately versioned projection/inbox identity or a controlled reset/migration plan. Keep the old projection available until the new one is verified when that is the chosen rollout.

State the recovery contract: authoritative source, available retention, schema compatibility, privacy/deletion policy, event ordering/version rules, and comparison checks. If facts have expired or were never published, offsets alone cannot rebuild them.
