# 8. Research and metrics: replace confident guesses with evidence

**Intermediate · about 2 hours with practice**

Plan a small usability study and interpret product metrics without confusing clicks with success.

## Use it

Design reviews, feature experiments, and analytics interpretation.

**Benefit:** Makes UX decisions explainable and revisable.

**Cost or limit:** Small studies and incomplete telemetry have limits.
