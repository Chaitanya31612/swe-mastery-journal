## Understand

An embedding maps an input into a numeric representation. Training encourages some relationships to appear as useful geometry.

Similar wording or meaning may place vectors near each other, depending on the model and task.

A vector is not a compressed database row that can reliably be decoded back into every original detail.

Distance functions differ. Cosine distance compares direction; dot product and Euclidean distance have different behavior and normalization assumptions.

Use the distance recommended for the selected embedding model and evaluate retrieval on your data.

Keep embedding model/version, dimensions, preprocessing, and distance choice with the index. Vectors from unrelated embedding models generally cannot be mixed just because they have the same dimension.

A migration needs re-embedding or a deliberately validated compatibility strategy. Similarity does not establish factual support or authorization.
