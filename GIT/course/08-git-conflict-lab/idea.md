## Understand the idea

Conflict markers identify text Git could not combine automatically. They do not tell you which product behavior should win.

Read the common base, each change, callers, and tests.

During rebase, Git replays your commits onto another base. The meanings of ours and theirs can feel reversed from an ordinary merge.

Inspect actual content rather than clicking a side label by habit.

Some dangerous conflicts are semantic and have no markers: one change renames a field while another still sends the old field; one adds a new state while another assumes only two states.

Test the combined contract even after a clean merge.
